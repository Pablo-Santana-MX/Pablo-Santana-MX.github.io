## 👥 Behavioral Segmentation
Understanding demographics and purchasing behavior is fundamental for targeted marketing. This portfolio documents the complete workflow, from data cleaning to final clustering, generating HTML reports as deliverables.

## 🛠️ Analytical Approach
*   **Unsupervised Learning:** Utilizing K-Means clustering algorithms to identify customer profiles based on annual income and spending scores.
*   **Reporting:** Automated generation of HTML reports to present segment characteristics to non-technical stakeholders.

## 🎯 Strategic Impact
By partitioning the customer base into defined segments, business strategies can be tailored to achieve higher conversion and retention rates through data-driven marketing campaigns.
