## 🌡️ The Urban Heat Island Phenomenon
Urban areas retain heat differently than their rural surroundings. This project analyzes the impact of urban development on the historical temperatures of Mérida, Yucatán, processing decades of climate data to visualize critical long-term trends.

## 🧠 Data Architecture
*   **Data Processing:** Cleaning and structuring the ERA5 time-series dataset (1950-2026) using Pandas and Numpy.
*   **Visualization:** Dynamic charts integrated into a Streamlit interface to demonstrate thermal anomalies and the Urban Heat Island effect.
