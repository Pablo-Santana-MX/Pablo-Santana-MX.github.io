## 🌌 Visualizing Stellar Formation
Data analysis extends far beyond standard business metrics. This project tackles the computational simulation of a Herbig-Haro object (protostar), with visual aesthetics and thermal modeling heavily inspired by the infrared imagery of the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST).

## 🧠 Architecture & Methodology
*   **Simulation Engine:** Utilizing Python's scientific stack (`numpy` and `matplotlib`) to efficiently calculate vectorized particle trajectories and render cinematic, perfectly looping animations.
*   **Applied Mathematics:** Designing computational algorithms to model magnetic shockwaves and fluid turbulence through composite wave interference, resolving underlying physical interactions.

## 📈 Analytical Impact
Developing high-fidelity physical simulations demonstrates deep abstraction capabilities, proving that algorithmic structuring and mathematical modeling skills translate effectively into highly complex problem domains and advanced data visualizations.
