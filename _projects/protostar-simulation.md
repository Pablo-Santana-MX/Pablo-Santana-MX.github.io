## 🌌 Visualizing Star Formation
This project features a computational simulation built in Python that models the formation of a protostar (Herbig-Haro object). The visual aesthetics and thermal modeling are heavily inspired by the spectacular infrared images captured by the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST).
