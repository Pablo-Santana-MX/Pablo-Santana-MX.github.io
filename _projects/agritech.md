**From Prediction to Prescription.** 

Traditional hydroponic systems often experience crop losses due to non-linear climatic deviations that standard linear models cannot predict or correct in time. To solve this, I developed an End-to-End analytical ecosystem:

* **Predictive Modeling:** Trained a *Random Forest* ensemble (94.5% Accuracy) to map the severe non-linear biological thresholds of crops (Lactuca sativa).
* **Explainable AI (XAI):** Audited telemetric variables from IoT sensors using Game Theory (*SHAP values*) to understand causal directionality.
* **Optimization Engine:** Implemented a heuristic search engine (*Grid Search*) that freezes uncontrollable weather variables and simulates thousands of biochemical scenarios (pH/EC) in milliseconds.
* **Operational Impact:** The system prescribes exact mechanical commands through an interactive "Digital Twin", allowing operators to transition from a crop stress state to the absolute global maximum yield in real-time.
